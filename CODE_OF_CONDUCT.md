# Code of Conduct

CropVector is a personal project by Ribin K Roy. It is not associated with a formal organization.

Maintainer and contributor profiles:

- [Ribin-K-Roy](https://github.com/Ribin-K-Roy)
- [Ryson-Theo](https://github.com/Ryson-Theo)

To maintain a positive and inclusive community, all contributors to CropVector are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful, kind, and patient when interacting with others.
- Welcome feedback and engage in constructive discussions.
- Avoid discriminatory, harassing, or offensive language or behavior.
- Focus on collaboration and problem-solving rather than criticism.
- Credit other contributors where due and be generous with acknowledgment.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the maintainer, Ribin K Roy, through this repository's issue tracker or another private channel. Provide as much detail as possible (who, what, when, and any relevant context or links).

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action, which may include a private discussion, removal of content, temporary suspension, or banning from the project, depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, chat rooms) and events where contributors represent the project.

## A Note From the Maintainers

Let's work together to make CropVector a project where everyone feels valued and supported. Thank you for contributing respectfully.
